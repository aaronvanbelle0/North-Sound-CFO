Common Questions

Everything you want
to know before starting.

Your bookkeeper records transactions. Your CPA files your taxes once a year. North Sound sits between those two and does something neither of them do: reads your numbers every month, explains what they mean in plain English, and tells you what to do about them. We are your strategic financial advisor — focused on helping you make better decisions, not on compliance or recordkeeping.

QuickBooks Online is ideal, but we can work with most accounting software. If your books are a mess, that is okay — we have seen worse. We will help you identify the quickest path to clean data. For the first report, just export whatever you have and we will work with it.

A full monthly report built from your actual QuickBooks data — revenue, gross profit, expense breakdown, AR aging, and a plain-English narrative explaining what we found. We also include a 45-minute call to walk through the findings. No sales pitch during the call — just an honest look at your numbers.

About 90 minutes total. Around the 25th of each month, you export two reports from QuickBooks — your P&L and AR aging, which takes about five minutes. We build the report, record a video walkthrough, and send it to you. You watch the video (8–12 minutes), then we have a 45-minute call.

The monthly option ($2,800/month) delivers a full CFO report every month with ongoing support, on-call access, and monthly 45-minute calls. The quarterly option ($2,800/quarter) is a deep-dive report and 90-minute strategy session four times per year — ideal for businesses that don't need monthly touchpoints but want serious CFO analysis on a regular cadence. Both include the same depth of analysis.

Our sweet spot is contractors with $500K–$12M in annual revenue — typically 3–50 employees. Large enough that the financials are complex and matter, small enough that you don't have a full-time CFO yet. If you are below $500K, the Monthly Report tier is a great starting point.

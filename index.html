Common Questions

Everything you want
to know before starting.

Your bookkeeper records transactions. Your CPA files your taxes once a year. ClearBooks sits between those two and does something neither of them do: reads your numbers every month, explains what they mean in plain English, and tells you what to do about them. We are your strategic financial advisor — focused on helping you make better decisions, not on compliance or recordkeeping.

QuickBooks Online is ideal, but we can work with most accounting software. If your books are a mess, that is okay — we have seen worse. We will help you identify the quickest path to clean data. For the first report, just export whatever you have and we will work with it. Part of what Month 1 does is identify what needs to be cleaned up.

A full monthly report built from your actual QuickBooks data — revenue, gross profit, expense breakdown, AR aging, and a plain-English narrative explaining what we found. We also include a 45-minute call to walk through the findings. No sales pitch during the call — just an honest look at your numbers. You will know whether we are a good fit after that conversation.

About 90 minutes total. Around the 25th of each month, you export two reports from QuickBooks — your P&L and AR aging, which takes about five minutes. We build the report, record a video walkthrough, and send it to you. You watch the video (8–12 minutes), then we have a 45-minute call. Everything else happens on our end.

Our focus is Whatcom and Skagit County because local knowledge matters — we know the local banks, the cost of materials, the competitive market, and the specific Washington state compliance issues relevant to Northwest Washington contractors. We do take on select clients in Island, San Juan, and Snohomish County when the fit is right. Reach out and we can talk.

Our sweet spot is contractors with $500K–$5M in annual revenue — typically 3–20 employees. Large enough that the financials are complex and matter, small enough that you do not have a full-time CFO yet. If you are below $500K, the Monthly Report tier is a great starting point. If you are above $5M, reach out to discuss what makes sense.
